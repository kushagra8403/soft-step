    Before you order

    How do I order?

    Add mats to your cart, enter your delivery details, pay by UPI and send the order to us on WhatsApp with one tap. We confirm your order and payment the same day.

    How do I pay?

    By UPI. Scan the QR at checkout or pay to panchaljelam@oksbi (Jelam Panchal) from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app. You can also pay to UPI number 90994 41429.

    Do you deliver across India?

    Yes. Delivery is free within Vadodara. For the rest of India, delivery charges are extra and we confirm them with you on call.

    Can I choose my colour?

    Yes. Colours depend on stock, so we confirm yours on WhatsApp before dispatch. If your colour is out of stock, we let you know before you pay.

    How do I wash my mat?

    Our mats are machine washable and quick-drying, with an anti-slip latex backing. Message us if you want care tips for a particular mat.

    Can I visit?

    Yes, by appointment in Vadodara. Message us on WhatsApp to set a time.
