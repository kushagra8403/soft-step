# Bug findings from the audit (checkout/core finder only; not yet fixed or independently verified)

### CORE-1 [medium] Any cart change after the pay step silently throws away the order, even after the customer has paid. 'Change something in this order' then makes a new order for the full amount
- file: softsteps.html:1512
- what: render() drops the pending order whenever the cart signature changes: `if(pend&&pend.sig!==sig()){pend=null;savePend()}`. It does this whether or not the customer has already paid by UPI (step 1) or sent the order (pend.sent). It gives no warning, only the normal 'Added …' toast. The pay step offers 'Change something in this order' (#editorder, line 1562) and keeps it visible after sending. That link goes back to the cart, and the next 'Continue to payment' creates a brand-new order id. The new WhatsApp text and UPI link ask for the full new total. Nothing refers to the order id or amount the customer already paid.
- repro: node /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/b3b2df08-4b5f-5d42-b6f7-07d0365cb062/scratchpad/audit2/work/core/c10_paid.cjs: pay step #SS-GP4XH9 for ₹249. Closing the drawer and adding one Aqua mat changes the bar from 'Finish order #SS-GP4XH9' to 'View cart · 2 items', sets ss_pending=null and gives no warning. Re-placing gives #SS-GP4YFH, ₹598, am=598, and the message does not mention SS-GP4XH9. In c8_amend.cjs, after Send on WhatsApp, the 'Change something' link is still visible. Using it gives new id #SS-GP0E93, asks for the full ₹997 instead of the ₹249 difference, and does not mention the first id.
- impact: A customer who pays ₹249 and then adds a mat is asked to pay ₹598 more. Many will pay the full amount again. Jelam gets two order ids and two UPI payments for overlapping goods, with nothing linking them, and has to sort out refunds or a double dispatch over WhatsApp.
- fix: Do not discard a pending order silently. When pend exists and the cart changes, keep its id as `replaces` and show an inline note: 'This updates order #SS-…'. Put 'Update to order SS-… (replaces it)' at the top of the new WhatsApp message. Ask on the pay step whether the earlier amount was already paid, and if so show and link only the balance (am = new total − paid). At minimum, show a toast or confirm-free inline warning before dropping a pending order whose pay step was shown.

### CORE-2 [medium] After an order is sent, the cart is never cleared, so the next visit's new mat is added on top of mats already ordered
- file: softsteps.html:1560
- what: Tapping 'Send order on WhatsApp' only sets pend.sent=true. The cart (ss_cart2) and the pending order stay for up to 7 days, and the bottom bar keeps saying 'Finish order #SS-…' as if the order were unfinished. Only the 'Start a new cart' button inside the sent box clears them. On a later visit, adding any item changes the signature. render() then silently drops the sent order, and the old (already ordered) lines stay in the cart with the new one. The bar now reads 'View cart · 3 items' and the next order includes the earlier mats again.
- repro: c4_nav.cjs scenario C: order Zigzag M ×2, tap Send (sent=true, sent box shown) and reload. The bar still says 'Finish order #SS-GOVDT0 ₹498'. Adding an Aqua mat gives bar 'View cart · 3 items ₹847', ss_pending null, and cart [zig M Forest ×2, sol M Aqua ×1]. The drawer now opens on the cart with both lines.
- impact: Returning customers easily place a duplicate order for mats they already bought and paid for. The 'Finish order' bar also suggests a completed order was not sent, which invites a second WhatsApp send of the same order.
- fix: When #sendwa is clicked, or when a pending order with sent=true is restored, mark the order as done. Show the bar as 'Order #… sent' (not 'Finish order'). On the next addToCart, start a fresh cart containing only the new item and toast 'Started a new cart, order #… was already sent'. Alternatively, clear the cart after send once the sent box has been shown, keeping a copy of the order text for 'copy again'.

### CORE-3 [medium] Vadodara-district PINs (391xxx) keep 'Vadodara – Free delivery', so the order says free delivery while the PIN note says charges apply. The delivery choice is never checked against the PIN
- file: features.js:429
- what: sync() maps 390xxx to 'vad' and other valid PINs (except 391) to 'out', but maps 391xxx to null, so the radio is left as it was. With the default ('vad') the note says 'Vadodara district: we'll confirm delivery charges on WhatsApp'. Meanwhile #tot shows no '+ delivery', the pay step says 'Pay ₹249' with no delivery suffix, and the WhatsApp text says 'Total: ₹249 (free delivery in Vadodara)'. If the visitor first typed a non-Vadodara PIN and then corrected it to 391xxx, the radio stays 'out' instead. #order-btn (softsteps.html 1539–1552) never compares dl() with the PIN, so a manual tap on 'Vadodara · Free' with a Mumbai PIN also produces 'free delivery in Vadodara'.
- repro: c2_pin.cjs: city Padra, PIN 391440 gives radio=vad, note 'Vadodara district: we'll confirm delivery charges…', pay amount '₹249' and message line 'Total: ₹249 (free delivery in Vadodara)' with address '…, Padra 391440'. Typing 400001 and then 391440 leaves radio=out.
- impact: Customers in Padra, Waghodia, Savli and other 391 PINs are told in their own order message that delivery is free. Jelam must then call to add charges the customer did not expect, which risks disputes or a lost sale.
- fix: Map 391xxx to 'out', or add a third 'district' state whose message reads '+ delivery (to be confirmed)'. In the #order-btn handler, recompute delivery from the PIN: if the PIN is not 390xxx and dl()==='vad', switch to 'out' (or show an inline error) before building the message.

### CORE-4 [medium] Android back button or iOS back swipe during checkout leaves the shop instead of closing the drawer or going back a step
- file: softsteps.html:1529
- what: openD(), step() and closeD() never touch history (no pushState or popstate anywhere in the snapshot). The drawer looks and behaves like a full-screen sheet on phones, but the system Back gesture navigates away from the page: to Instagram or WhatsApp, or it closes the Custom Tab.
- repro: c4_nav.cjs scenario A: open the shop from a referrer page, add a mat, open the drawer, go to Details and type a name, then call page.goBack(). The URL becomes the referrer ('Instagram'). On Forward the drawer is closed.
- impact: On the main visitor device (mid-range Android), pressing Back to return from Details to the cart, or to dismiss the sheet, takes the buyer off the site mid-checkout. That is a direct drop-off point.
- fix: In openD push a state (history.pushState({ss:'drawer',v}, '')), and push again in step() for det and pay. Add a popstate listener that goes back a step or calls closeD() when the state is not ours. When closing through ✕, Escape or the scrim, call history.back() if the current state is ours so entries do not pile up. Guard all of it with try/catch for the sandboxed artifact frame.

### CORE-5 [medium] A cart saved in the browser keeps its old price, size text and colour, so after any price change returning visitors are charged the old price
- file: softsteps.html:1569
- what: Restore pushes stored lines unchanged (`if(x&&P[x.k]&&x.q>0)cart.push(x)`). The unit price x.p, the size label and the colour are never re-derived from P or checked against existing swatches. sig() leaves out the price, so a pending order saved before a price change is also restored with its old msg and total. Lines whose stored id differs from what addToCart builds are never merged.
- repro: c6_store.cjs case 1: seed ss_cart2 with Zigzag M ×2 at p:199 (page price ₹249). Cart bar, pay step and UPI link all show ₹398 (am=398), and the message reads '× 2 = ₹398'. Adding the same mat again creates a second line at ₹249 instead of merging.
- impact: The first time Jelam changes a price, every returning customer's cart (on the artifact URL, which keeps storage across republishes, and on Netlify) shows the old price. The WhatsApp order and UPI amount follow it, while the product cards show the new one. Discontinued colours also stay orderable.
- fix: On restore, rebuild each line from P. Parse the size letter from x.id or x.size and look it up in SIZEIDX, and require SS.swatch(k, colour) to exist. Set p = P[k].sizes[i][3], size = letter + ' ' + dims and id = k|letter|colour, and coerce q to an integer from 1 to 99. Merge duplicates, drop anything invalid, save the cleaned cart, and include the price in sig().

### CORE-6 [medium] One malformed stored cart line crashes the core script at startup and leaves the whole shop broken on every visit
- file: softsteps.html:1515
- what: The restore filter accepts any object with a known k and q>0. For a line without a numeric p (for example a future schema with 'price', or p:null), render() throws in inr(x.p) at line 1571, before `ready=true` and `window.SS=…`. Every add-on (features, fx, shop, pairs, tryon) returns early because SS is undefined. Cart lines are never drawn, so they cannot be removed. Because render() throws before save(), the bad data is never overwritten and the page stays broken after every reload. A corrupted ss_pending missing t has a similar problem: pend is assigned before fillPay throws, leaving a half-filled pay step. A q stored as a string gives 'View cart · 02 items'.
- repro: c7_brick.cjs: ss_cart2=[{k:'sol',…,price:349,q:1}] or p:null gives the page error "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'toLocaleString')", typeof window.SS === 'undefined', bar '₹NaN' (or ₹0), and 0 cart lines after tapping Add. The page is still broken after a reload. c6_store.cjs case 3 covers q:"2" (shows '02 items').
- impact: This is latent today because all versions write the same shape. But any future data change without a key bump, or a stray edit, turns the shop into a dead page with a cart that cannot be emptied for every affected returning visitor, and only clearing site data fixes it.
- fix: Use the CORE-5 validation (rebuild from P, check types). In addition, wrap the initial restore + render in try/catch: on error, reset cart=[] and remove ss_cart2 and ss_pending, then render. In the ss_pending restore, require typeof t==='number' and string oid/msg, and assign pend only after fillPay succeeds.

### CORE-7 [low] Checkout PIN field cuts a pasted or autofilled '390 007' to '390 00' and then rejects it
- file: softsteps.html:1315
- what: #o-pin has maxlength=6 and no input or paste cleaning. The validator `/^[1-9]\d{5}$/` does not strip spaces. Indian addresses commonly write the PIN as '390 007'. maxlength truncates it before any script runs, so the field ends up as '390 00'. features.js already handles this exact case for the standalone checker #pin-in (lines 407–414, with a comment explaining it) but not for the checkout field.
- repro: c2_pin.cjs: focus #o-pin and insertText('390 007'). The value becomes '390 00', the PIN note is blank, and 'Continue to payment' shows 'Enter your 6-digit PIN code.'. Autofill truncation to maxlength is from code reading and known Chrome behaviour; it was not exercised headless.
- impact: Buyers who paste their PIN or have it saved with a space get an error they cannot see the cause of ('390 00' looks like they typed it wrong). This adds friction at the last form step.
- fix: Attach the same input and paste sanitiser used for #pin-in to #o-pin: strip non-digits, then slice to 6. Or drop maxlength and validate pin.replace(/\D/g,'').

### CORE-8 [low] Two open tabs overwrite each other's cart
- file: softsteps.html:1504
- what: The cart is read from localStorage once at load. save() then writes the whole in-memory array, and there is no 'storage' event listener. A tab opened earlier (for example from a WhatsApp or Instagram link) that adds one item replaces the cart another tab built.
- repro: c4_nav.cjs scenario B: tab A adds Zigzag M and Solid L, then tab B (opened before) adds Chenille XL. After reloading, tab A's cart is only ['chen|XL|Navy stripe'].
- impact: Items silently disappear from the cart. The buyer may not notice and order less than intended, or lose a cart they spent time building.
- fix: Listen for window 'storage' events on ss_cart2 and ss_pending, reload the cart from storage (with CORE-5 validation) and re-render. Or have addToCart and the steppers re-read storage before modifying (read-modify-write).

### CORE-9 [low] Double taps act on the next control: Remove deletes two lines, and 'Continue to delivery details' shows an error at once
- file: softsteps.html:1521
- what: With width=device-width there is no double-tap zoom on Android Chrome, so a double tap delivers two clicks. Each cart action re-renders synchronously and lines shift up. The second tap then lands on the next line's Remove or − button. #go switches to Details at once, and #order-btn sits under the same spot (go 20–370 × 776–828, order-btn 109–370 × 776–828 at 390px). The second tap therefore runs validation on an empty form. Add to cart has no guard either, so a double tap adds 2.
- repro: c3_taps.cjs: with 3 lines, a touchscreen double tap on the first Remove leaves only ['Aqua'] (two lines removed), and a double tap on Add gives count 2. c11_go2.cjs: a double tap on 'Continue to delivery details' shows 'Add your name.' with aria-invalid and focus in #o-name (keyboard opens).
- impact: Buyers remove items they wanted, or see an error before typing anything. On a phone-first shop with many first-time online buyers this causes confusion and wrong orders.
- fix: Ignore clicks on #v-cart buttons, #order-btn and the [data-add] buttons for about 350 ms after a re-render or step change (store a timestamp in render() and step() and check it in the handlers). Or debounce per button.

### CORE-10 [low] Copy buttons get stuck on 'Copied', say 'Copied' when copying failed, and drop focus out of the drawer
- file: softsteps.html:1555
- what: copyText() saves btn.textContent as the 'original' on every call. A second tap within 1.5 s therefore saves 'Copied', and the label never returns to 'Copy UPI ID' or 'Copy the order text'. The fallback ignores the return value of document.execCommand('copy') and calls done() anyway, so a failed copy still shows 'Copied'. It also focuses the temporary textarea and removes it, leaving focus on <body> inside the modal drawer. features.js dlgCopy already has the _cp guard, checks ok and refocuses the button; the core does none of this.
- repro: c3_taps.cjs: a double tap on 'Copy UPI ID' leaves the label 'Copied' 3.5 s later, and two clicks on #copyorder do the same. c9_copy.cjs: with clipboard.writeText rejecting and execCommand returning false, the label is 'Copied' with no toast and focus on BODY. With a working fallback, focus is still BODY. The iOS readonly-textarea select() failure that would trigger this in practice is known platform behaviour and was not reproduced (no WebKit here).
- impact: On the 'WhatsApp not opening?' path the buyer is told the order text was copied when it may not have been, and pastes nothing or something stale into WhatsApp. Keyboard users lose their place in the checkout dialog.
- fix: Reuse dlgCopy's logic in copyText. Ignore re-entry while btn._cp is set, check `ok = document.execCommand('copy')`, call done() only if ok, otherwise toast 'Couldn't copy, long-press to select: …', and refocus btn after removing the textarea.

### CORE-11 [low] Delivery details are lost on a reload or when Android discards the tab mid-checkout
- file: softsteps.html:1539
- what: Name, phone, address, city, PIN, email, gift note and delivery choice are never saved. Only the cart and a placed (pay-step) order are kept. After a reload the drawer is closed and every field on the Details step is empty. Chrome did not restore them.
- repro: c4_nav.cjs scenario D: fill Details (Mumbai 400001) and reload. Drawer closed, bar 'View cart · 1 item', #o-name is ''. Reloading on the Pay step restores correctly ('Finish order #…', same oid).
- impact: On mid-range Android, switching to WhatsApp or Contacts to copy an address often gets the Chrome tab discarded. The buyer comes back to an empty form and has to retype everything at the most drop-off-prone step.
- fix: Keep a details draft in localStorage (try/catch, on input), restore it into the fields on load, and clear it when the order is placed or 'Start a new cart' is used. Optionally reopen at the Details step if a draft exists and the cart is unchanged.

### CORE-12 [low] 'Browse mats' in the empty cart smooth-scrolls even with reduced motion
- file: softsteps.html:1517
- what: #browse calls `$("#shop").scrollIntoView({behavior:"smooth"})` unconditionally. Every other scroll in features.js goes through behavior(), which returns 'auto' under prefers-reduced-motion.
- repro: c9_copy.cjs: context reducedMotion 'reduce', empty the cart and tap 'Browse mats'. scrollY samples every 60 ms are 10 96 220 467 795 1075 1455 1719, an animated scroll.
- impact: This breaks the shop's reduced-motion rule. A long animated scroll can trigger discomfort for motion-sensitive visitors.
- fix: Use behavior: matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches ? 'auto' : 'smooth' (or SS-shared behavior()).
